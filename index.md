# Privacy Policy for Junk Mail

**Last updated: April 22, 2026**

Junk Mail is a personal photo journal app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

Junk Mail does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

## Data Storage

All journal entries, photos, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

## Permissions

Junk Mail may request access to your camera and photo library solely to let you add photos to your journal. This media is saved only to your device and is never uploaded or shared.

## Third-Party Services

Junk Mail does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporting services.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
